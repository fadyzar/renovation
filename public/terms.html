1. What MGbit is

MGbit is an online marketplace. Property owners (“Owners”) post renovation projects. Independent contractors (“Contractors”) submit quotes, communicate with Owners, and are paid through the platform in milestones.

MGbit is not a contractor, does not perform renovation work, and is not a party to the agreement between an Owner and a Contractor. Contractors are independent businesses, not MGbit employees or agents. Each Contractor alone is responsible for the work it performs, including its quality, safety, timeliness, permits and compliance with law. MGbit facilitates payments between Owners and Contractors as described in Section 5.

5. Payments, milestones and fees

  • Free to use: creating an account, posting projects, receiving quotes and submitting bids are free.
  • Milestone payments: an accepted project is split into payment milestones. The Owner pays each milestone by card through our payment processor, Stripe. The first milestone is paid to start the project.
  • Funds held until approval: MGbit holds milestone funds and releases them to the Contractor after the Owner approves the milestone. If the Owner neither approves nor requests a revision by the review deadline shown in the Services, the milestone may be approved automatically. MGbit is not a bank or escrow agent, and funds held pending approval do not earn interest.
  • Platform fee: MGbit charges Contractors a platform commission, currently 10% of the project total. It is deducted from the Contractor’s payouts. Owners pay the quoted price and are not charged an additional platform fee. We may change our fees for future projects with notice.
  • Payouts: Contractor payouts are sent by bank transfer to the account you provide, after milestone approval. They may be delayed by a dispute, chargeback, suspected fraud, missing payout information, or legal requirements.
  • Payment authorization: by paying, you authorize the charge to your payment method. Your use of Stripe is also subject to Stripe’s terms.

  • All amounts are in U.S. dollars. Each user is responsible for their own taxes.
